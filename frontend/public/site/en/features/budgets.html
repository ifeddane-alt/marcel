Module 04 · Budgets & finance

The budget landing, before the landing.

CAPEX, OPEX, consumed, EAC, ETC: MARCEL tracks financial steering continuously, from project to portfolio — and surfaces drifts before they become crisis topics.

04.1

From project to portfolio, the same numbers

Each project carries its CAPEX/OPEX envelope, consumed, EAC and ETC. Programs and the portfolio aggregate automatically — the finance department and project managers read the same reality.

  • Budget dashboard — CAPEX consumed, OPEX consumed, EAC vs planned, ETC vs EAC KPIs with progress rings.
  • Automatic aggregation — per program and portfolio, without manual consolidation.
  • Consumption fed by actuals — validated timesheets × day rates + external costs: no double entry.
04.2

Drifts surface on their own

When a project's EAC crosses its envelope, MARCEL flags it: in red across the views, by email if configured, and in the AI agent's recommendations. You arbitrate while it is still a choice.

  • EAC overrun alert — configurable tolerance threshold, notification on crossing, built-in anti-spam.
  • Traced budget revisions — every envelope change is historised: who, when, why.
  • Variances visible at every level — project tile, budget page, dashboard, executive export: the red is the same everywhere.
04.3

External costs under control

Time-and-materials or fixed-price engagements, contractual day rates, invoices: MARCEL reconciles what was signed with what is invoiced, vendor by vendor.

  • T&M & fixed price — consumption tracked per contract, alerts on fixed-price contracts nearing exhaustion.
  • Contractual vs invoiced rates — variance surfaces automatically, line by line.
  • Contract expiries — D-90/60/30/7 alerts on vendor contracts, same as for resources.
