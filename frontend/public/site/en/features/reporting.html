Module 06 · Reporting & integrations

Every audience gets its reporting. No re-entry.

A customisable dashboard for the day-to-day, a weekly PDF for the board, PowerPoint for committees, Power BI for analysts — everything comes from the same data, in one click.

06.1

A dashboard that looks like you

KPIs, budget, milestones, team workload, risks, contracts to renew: 22 blocks to activate, move and resize by drag & drop. Each user composes their cockpit — the PMO does not see the same screen as the CFO, by design.

  • Drag & drop grid — compose your screen, it is remembered per user.
  • Ready-made business blocks — milestone gauge, top projects, recent decisions, pending timesheets, contracts within 60 days…
  • Weekly executive PDF — the portfolio summary ready to send, generated from the dashboard.
06.2

Open to your BI landscape

MARCEL does not replace your analysis tools: it feeds them. Power BI kit with pre-configured template, generalised Excel exports across 9 sections, webhooks and email alerts to your channels.

  • Power BI kit — 6 feeds (projects, resources, timesheets, budget, risks, milestones), dedicated API key, .zip template.
  • Excel both ways — readable export (currency formats, dates, help sheet) and import with preview.
  • Webhooks & email alerts — project events, budget thresholds, contracts: pushed to your tools, HMAC-signed.
06.3

The AI PMO agent, your portfolio analyst

Ask your questions in English or French: the agent cross-references budgets, milestones, risks and capacity to answer — with its sources. It also detects anomalies on its own: EAC drifts, slipping milestones, overloaded teams.

  • Natural-language questions — "which projects are at risk of overrunning their budget this quarter?"
  • Exportable recommendations — anomaly summaries as PDF or Excel, ready for the steering committee agenda.
  • Usage analytics — questions, costs and AI performance tracked for the administrator.
