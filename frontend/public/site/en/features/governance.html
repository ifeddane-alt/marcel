05.1

Decisions that leave a trace

Who decided what, when, on which project, and where does the action stand? The decision log answers — and every decision stays attached to its context: budget at the time, milestones, open risks.

  • Centralised log — timestamped steering and executive decisions, linked to projects, with action follow-up.
  • Qualified demands — every project demand follows a workflow: qualification, prioritisation, acceptance, conversion.
  • Instant search — retrieve a decision by project, date or keyword through global search.
05.2

Arbitrate on scenarios, not intuitions

The trade-off module scores projects against your weighted criteria, simulates budget envelopes and compares two scenarios side by side: what happens if we postpone X, cut Y, accelerate Z?

  • Weighted scoring — value, risk, strategic alignment: your criteria, your weights, an objectivised ranking.
  • Envelope simulator — distribute the budget across programs and visualise what fits and what falls.
  • Compared scenarios — two hypotheses displayed side by side, ready to present to the committee.
05.3

Risks, compliance and scaled agile

Risks are mapped as probability × impact with mitigation plans. Regulatory milestones get dedicated tracking. And SAFe organisations find their trains, PIs and capabilities.

  • Risk heatmap — probability × impact, computed criticality, critical risks surfaced to the dashboard.
  • Regulatory milestones — tracked in a dedicated family, visible in the portfolio compliance view.
  • Branded executive export — your organisation's logo, colours and fonts on every slide.
  • SAFe trains — PIs, sprints and capabilities linked to projects and the portfolio.
