Frequently asked questions

What we get asked before the demo.

SaaS or on-premise: how does MARCEL deploy?

Both. As multi-tenant SaaS, your data is strictly isolated per tenant. On-premise, MARCEL installs on your own servers (Docker) with a dedicated licence — your data never leaves your infrastructure.

How long does it take to start with our existing portfolio?

Hours, not months. Your Excel repositories (projects, resources, teams, budgets) import with a preview before commit, and your Microsoft Project schedules (.mpp) load natively. Most teams work on their real data within the first week.

Does MARCEL integrate with our tools (Power BI, Excel, SSO)?

Yes. Ready-to-use Power BI kit (6 feeds + template), generalised Excel import/export, Microsoft Project import, signed webhooks and email alerts. For authentication: SSO with Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID or SAML 2.0, with fine-grained role permissions.

How does the AI agent work? Is our data used to train models?

The AI agent answers your questions by cross-referencing only your tenant's data (budgets, milestones, risks, capacity) and cites its sources. Your data is not used to train models and does not leave your perimeter.

What is the pricing?

Pricing depends on your scope (number of users, SaaS or on-premise). We do not publish a public price list: request a 30-minute demo and leave with a proposal tailored to your context.

In which languages is MARCEL available?

The interface is fully bilingual French / English, at each user's choice. Exports (PowerPoint, PDF, Excel) respect your organisation's language and branding.
