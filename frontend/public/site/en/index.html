The platform

One tool for the entire lifecycle of your projects.

From initial demand to executive trade-off: every module speaks the same visual language — tiles, progress rings and green / amber / red statuses readable in one second.

Portfolio & programs

A consolidated view of all your projects as rich tiles: progress, budget, key milestones, risks and RAG status. Programs automatically aggregate their projects, with upcoming milestones right on each tile.

CAPEX / OPEX / EAC budgets

Envelopes, consumed, estimate at completion (EAC) and estimate to complete (ETC) tracked continuously by project, program and portfolio. Overruns turn red before they become boardroom topics.

Resources & capacity

Teams × months cross-view over 3 to 12 months, overload alerts, contracts and expiry dates. Excel export of the matrix for your committees.

Governance & executive

Decisions, trade-offs, regulatory compliance and branded PowerPoint exports: your committee deck prepares itself.

Excel & MS Project import

Your .mpp schedules and Excel repositories import in seconds, with preview and control before committing.

Timesheets & workflows

Time entry by project, 2- or 3-level validation workflows, reminders and automatic consolidation into budgets. Project demands follow a qualification workflow all the way to project conversion.
